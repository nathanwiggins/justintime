COMPLIANCE NOTES
- Follow exact alphanumeric section layout of NSF 1030 budget form (A–I)
- Domestic (E.1) and Foreign (E.2) travel must be explicitly segregated
- Participant Support Costs (F) are exempt from Indirect Costs (F&A)
- Participant Support sub-sections: Stipends (F.1), Travel (F.2), Subsistence (F.3), Other (F.4)
- Typically limited to 5 pages unless the solicitation specifies otherwise

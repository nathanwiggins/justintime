General grants.gov Budget Justification

================================================================
COMPLIANCE NOTES
- Section layout: A. Personnel, B. Fringe Benefits, C. Travel, D. Equipment,
  E. Supplies, F. Contractual, G. Construction, H. Other, I. Indirect Costs, J. Total Costs
- Personnel (A) combines Senior Personnel and Other Personnel under one header,
  with a single combined "total request for Personnel" summary line
- Travel (C) lists Domestic and Foreign travel under one header, no sub-headers
- Contractual (F) lists Consultant Services and Subawards under one header, no
  sub-headers, with a single combined "total request for Contracts" summary line
- Construction (G) categories follow the federal SF-424C Budget Information for
  Construction Programs form (site work, demolition/removal, land/structures/
  rights-of-way, architectural/engineering fees, project inspection fees,
  construction/major renovation, contingencies) — modeled as a lightweight
  array of cost items, not the full 17-field SF-424C form
- Other (H) combines Participant Support Costs (H.1), Publication Costs /
  Documentation / Dissemination (H.2), Computer Services (H.3), and Other (H.4)
- Total Costs (J) is a computed grand total across sections A-I, not AI-generated
================================================================

NIH Modular Budget Justification

================================================================
COMPLIANCE NOTES — READ CAREFULLY
- NEVER mention specific salary amounts, fringe rates, or category totals
- Personnel justification must focus entirely on names, roles, and effort (person months)
- If a subawardee is included: state the institution name, declare domestic or foreign,
  and list subaward personnel names, roles, and person months only
- Additional Narrative is required ONLY if module counts vary across budget years
  (e.g., 4 modules in Year 1, 3 in Year 2 due to equipment purchase)
================================================================

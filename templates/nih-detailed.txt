COMPLIANCE NOTES
- Effort must be described using "person months" (Calendar, Academic, or Summer)
- Percentage effort is obsolete — do not use it
- No hard page limit, but justifications must remain concise
- If a subaward exists, its direct AND indirect costs must be clearly noted
- A separate subaward budget justification must follow the main narrative
